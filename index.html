Use your curve in After Effects

Works with numeric properties such as Position, Scale, Rotation, Opacity, and sliders.

  1. Create at least two keyframes on your property in After Effects.
  2. Click Copy AE Expression below.
  3. Alt-click the property stopwatch on Windows, or Option-click on Mac.
  4. Paste into the expression field, then click outside it and preview.

Keyframe spacing controls duration in AE; the duration and blur controls above are preview-only. This expression replaces easing in the chosen intervals. Position moves in a straight line between keyframe values; existing curved motion paths and Hold interpolation are not preserved. Text and shape paths are not supported. Outside the chosen intervals, your original animation is retained.
